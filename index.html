    // SYSTEM VERIFICATION
    
    
    Does it work? Validation against 36 established analog sites
    
    
    A trustworthy analog finder must rediscover the places NASA, ESA, CSA, ISRO, CNSA and the Austrian Space Forum already use. For each target we take the established field sites whose environment matches its category (polar & ice sites for the lunar south pole, arid & evaporite sites for Mars sedimentary basins, volcanic & lava-tube sites for lunar maria and pits) and ask: how often does a known analog outscore a random land cell? That probability is the ROC AUC — 0.5 is chance, 1.0 is perfect.
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
    
    
    
    
      
    AUC by mission objective
    
      
    
      
    Negative controls (Amazon, Congo, Paris, Singapore) rank below the 50th percentile for every Mars target — checked in the automated test-suite (tests/engine.test.mjs). Sites that score low are informative too: Rio Tinto and Pavilion Lake are process analogs (acid mineralogy, microbialites) rather than environmental analogs, and small islands/peaks (Mauna Kea, Teide) are averaged with their wet lowlands at 0.5° resolution.
